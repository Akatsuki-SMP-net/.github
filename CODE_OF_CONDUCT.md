# Code of Conduct

AKATSUKI SMP aims to be a respectful, safe, and constructive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and differing opinions.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Respect other people's privacy.

## Reporting problems

Report serious or repeated problems privately to the project maintainers or organization owners. Do not post private information publicly.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
